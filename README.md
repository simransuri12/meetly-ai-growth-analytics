# Meetly AI — Understanding SaaS Growth Through Data

**A hands-on portfolio project exploring how a SaaS product attracts users, turns them into active customers, and grows over time.**

## What is Meetly AI?

Meetly AI is a fictional AI-powered personal assistant designed for busy professionals.

Imagine having one assistant that helps you stay on top of emails, prepares you for meetings, remembers important conversations, organizes your tasks, and reminds you about things before they slip through the cracks.

The idea behind Meetly AI is simple: help people spend less time managing their work and more time actually doing it.

I chose this product concept because it gives me an opportunity to explore several interesting growth and product analytics challenges.

## Why this project?

Getting people to sign up for a product is only the beginning. The real challenge is getting them to experience its value, come back regularly, and eventually become paying customers.

For this project, I want to look beyond signups and explore the complete customer journey.

Some of the questions I want to answer are:

- Which marketing channels attract users who actually engage with the product?
- At what stage are new users dropping off during onboarding?
- Does showing users a personalized demo before asking them to connect their apps improve activation?
- Which features encourage users to return?
- What separates users who become paying customers from those who leave?
- Where should the growth team focus its time and marketing budget?

Rather than just calculating metrics, I want to understand what the numbers mean and how they can inform business decisions.

## A closer look at the onboarding experiment

One question I'm particularly interested in is whether users should connect their apps before experiencing the product, or see what Meetly AI can do for them first.

I'll compare two approaches:

- **Option A — Connect first:** Ask users to connect an app before showing them a personalized demo.
- **Option B — Demo first:** Give users a personalized preview of the assistant before asking them to connect an app.

The main metric will be the percentage of users who become activated within seven days of signing up.

I'll also look at onboarding completion, app connections, and time to first value to understand what's happening along the way.

## How will I define activation?

For this project, a user is considered activated when they experience at least one successful, meaningful outcome within seven days of signing up.

For example:
- Receiving a personalized daily plan
- Sending an AI-assisted email reply
- Receiving a meeting summary
- Getting a predictive reminder

Simply signing up, viewing a demo, or connecting an app won't count as activation. I want to measure whether users actually experience the value of the product.
